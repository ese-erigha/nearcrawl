# Home country control

The home list can hold one country or several. The device country starts selected. Each added country loads that country's hot list. The panel merges the rows, keeps breaking first, then shares, then time, and stops at 20. Breaking and Also hot filter that merged list.

One selected country turns the globe to face it. Two or more frame a box around the set. The ticker and the dock stay the worldwide breaking feed.

`[ Canada x ]` and `[ Japan x ]` are the selected countries. Canada is the device country on the first load. `[ v ]` adds a country. `[x]` on a chip removes that country. `[x]` at the top closes the left panel. The globe stays.

`(14d)` is the selected window. `( All 12 )` is the selected topic. `*` is a filled dot. `o` is a hollow dot. A press on Breaking or Also hot keeps that status inside the merged list. A second press clears it.

Each country read stops at 20 stories before the merge. A story past that cut stays out.

The live line in the top bar is worldwide breaking news. The dock label is "Global Breaking News". `<` and `>` scroll those cards. A country change does not reload the ticker or the dock.
